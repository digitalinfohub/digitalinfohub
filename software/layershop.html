A layer workflow that actually helps

The same habits that keep a real edit editable.

One job per layerPhoto on the bottom, retouching above it, text on top. Deleting a bad experiment then costs one click instead of an undo marathon.
Blend, do not eraseSet a texture layer to Multiply or Screen and pull the opacity down. You keep the pixels and can change your mind later.
Adjust the copyDuplicate a layer before heavy colour work so the original stays untouched underneath — that is what non-destructive means in practice.
