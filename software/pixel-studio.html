Pixel art is a constraint game

Fewer pixels means every one has to earn its place.

Start at 16×16If the silhouette does not read at 16 pixels, it will not read at 64. Block the shape first, add detail second.
Pick three shadesBase, shadow and highlight. More tones blur together at small sizes and make the sprite look muddy.
Use mirror modeFaces, ships and shields are symmetrical. Draw half, mirror it, then break the symmetry deliberately somewhere.
