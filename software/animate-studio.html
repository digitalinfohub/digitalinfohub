Animation that reads well

Three rules that matter more than drawing skill.

Plan the key posesDraw the start, the middle and the end first. Everything between them is easier once the extremes exist and the timing is decided.
Use onion skinFaint ghosts of the previous and next frame show how far a shape should travel. Big jumps feel fast, small ones feel gentle.
12 fps is enoughHand-drawn animation has lived at 12 frames per second for a century. Fewer frames means more expression per drawing.
