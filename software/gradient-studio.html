Tints & shades

Gradients that do not look like 2010

Small decisions that separate a designer gradient from a default one.

Shift the midpointMoving the second stop to 40–60% controls where the blend happens. A midpoint near 35% keeps the first colour present longer.
Stay in one hue familyTwo neighbouring hues plus a lighter tint reads as light. Blue to orange reads as a poster — sometimes that is the point.
Check the text on topGradients change contrast across the surface. If white text sits on it, test the lightest end, not the average.
