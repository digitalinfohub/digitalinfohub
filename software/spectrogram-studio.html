Spectrogram Music Maker

Most music software hides sound behind piano rolls. Here you draw straight onto the spectrum: time runs left to right, pitch runs bottom to top, brightness is loudness. Hit play and your drawing becomes a synth pattern.

How to get something musical out of it

Three patterns that sound good immediately.

Draw chordsPaint three stacked dots in the same column. Because each column is a step, vertical stacks become chords and horizontal runs become melodies.
Make a basslineKeep the bottom quarter busy and leave the top sparse. Low partials carry rhythm, high partials read as sparkle.
Trace a real trackLoad any audio file and its spectrum appears behind the grid. Draw over the bassline or the melody you can see, and you get a playable version of it. The microphone is optional — use it to see your own voice instead.
