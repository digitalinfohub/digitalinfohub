Chord formulas, in plain words

Intervals are measured from the root.

Major vs minorMajor is 1–3–5. Lower the third by one semitone and you get minor: 1–b3–5. That single note carries most of the emotion.
Seventh chordsAdd the seventh on top. Major 7 sounds dreamy, dominant 7 sounds like it wants to resolve, minor 7 is the jazz default.
SuspendedSus2 and sus4 replace the third with the second or fourth. Neither major nor minor — useful when a melody already carries the mood.
