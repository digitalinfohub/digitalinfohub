WebP to PNG.

Convert WebP images into standard PNG files without uploading them anywhere. Everything is decoded and re-encoded in this tab.

PNG is lossless, so the converted file is often larger than the original WebP. That is expected — PNG is the safer format for editing, apps and older software that cannot open WebP.

Why convert?Some apps, print workflows and older browsers still refuse WebP. PNG opens everywhere.
