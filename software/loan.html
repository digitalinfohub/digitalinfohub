Questions people ask

Does an extra payment really help that much?

Yes, because interest is charged on the remaining balance. On a long loan, paying a little extra every month can cut years off the term and thousands in interest. Watch the “interest saved” figure change as you move the slider.

Why is early repayment mostly interest?

Interest is calculated on the balance, which is largest at the start. As the balance falls, more of each payment goes to principal — that is why the interest line shrinks over time.

What about a variable rate?

Run the calculation at two or three rates to see the range. A 1% change on a large, long loan is usually a meaningful monthly difference, so budget for the higher figure.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an arithmetic tool. Lenders add fees, insurance and their own rounding; use their key facts document for the binding numbers.
