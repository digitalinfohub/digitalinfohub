Recording something useful

Small habits that save a lot of re-recording.

Record a tab, not a screenTab capture avoids notifications, other windows and desktop clutter. It also produces a much smaller file.
Prepare before you hit recordClose what you do not need, set the zoom level and have the first click ready. The first ten seconds are always the worst.
Narrate while it happensMicrophone audio makes a recording feel like a walkthrough instead of surveillance. One sentence per action is plenty.
