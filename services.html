Questions

Before you get in touch

What counts as an edit or revision?

Any change requested after the first version is delivered: wording, images, colours, layout tweaks or a new version of a graphic. Each round is charged at the per-revision rate shown on that service. Grouping your feedback into one round keeps the cost down.

Why is hosting only £40 a year?

The sites we build are front-end only and hosted without a traditional server, so there is no monthly server bill to pass on. The annual fee covers the domain sourcing, deployment and ongoing management. Bigger builds are quoted separately because scope varies.

Do you need my social media password?

No. For management on your own channels we ask for an access grant or a page invite through the platform itself. You keep ownership and can remove access at any time.

Are the free tools really free?

Yes. The browser apps, tech news and digital strategy guidance are free for everyone. Digital Infohub is supported by advertising revenue, so you never need an account or a subscription to use them.

Can I advertise without buying anything else?

Yes. Website advertising is a standalone placement: £100 per year for a homepage sponsorship listing or a homepage banner ad. Tell us which format you want in the contact form.
