Newest Apple Watch 2026: Series 12 and Ultra 4 Explained

Find out which Apple Watch is newest in 2026 and discover the latest features of Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4.

Apple introduced its latest smartwatch generation in September 2026, bringing significant improvements to health monitoring, fitness tracking and everyday functionality. The lineup continues to split between the mainstream Series models and the rugged Ultra series.

Apple Watch Series 12

The Series 12 is Apple's latest mainstream smartwatch, designed for everyday users who want comprehensive health monitoring, seamless notifications and robust fitness features.

Key features include:

  • New S11 processor: Delivers faster app loading and smoother interface interactions.
  • Improved heart-rate sensing: More accurate readings during intense workouts and rest.
  • Higher-frequency heart-rate variability measurements: Provides deeper insights into stress and recovery.
  • New readiness score: A daily metric that helps you decide how hard to push your workout based on your body's current state.
  • Advanced fitness tracking: Enhanced algorithms for running, cycling and swimming metrics.
  • Improved durability: Stronger glass and more resistant casing for daily wear and tear.

Apple Watch Ultra 4

The Apple Watch Ultra 4 is Apple's premium adventure-focused smartwatch. It is designed for users who need additional durability, extended battery life, outdoor functionality and advanced fitness tracking for extreme conditions.

With a larger, brighter display and specialized action buttons, the Ultra 4 remains the top choice for triathletes, hikers and divers.

Which Apple Watch Should You Buy?

  • Choose the Series 12 if you want a sleek, lightweight everyday smartwatch that handles health tracking and notifications perfectly.
  • Choose the Ultra 4 if you need a more rugged device for outdoor activities, demanding workouts, or if you prioritize maximum battery life and screen visibility in bright sunlight.

Final Thoughts

Apple's latest smartwatch generation focuses on improving health insights and making everyday fitness tracking more useful. Whether you choose the balanced Series 12 or the extreme-ready Ultra 4, both models offer the best wearable experience Apple has ever produced.

Apple Watch Series 12

Series 12 是 Apple 最新的 mainstream 智能手表，专为希望获得全面健康监测、无缝通知和强大健身功能的日常用户而设计。

主要功能包括：

  • 全新 S11 处理器：提供更快的应用加载速度和更流畅的界面交互。
  • 改进的心率感应：在剧烈运动和休息期间提供更准确的读数。

Apple Watch Ultra 4 是 Apple 面向冒险的高端智能手表。它专为需要额外耐用性、延长电池寿命、户外功能以及在极端条件下进行高级健身追踪的用户而设计。
