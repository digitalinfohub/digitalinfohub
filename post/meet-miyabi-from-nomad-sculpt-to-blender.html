Meet Miyabi: From Nomad Sculpt to Blender

Every great 3D artist starts somewhere, and Miyabi's journey has already been an impressive one.

Based in the United Kingdom, Miyabi first developed her skills using Nomad Sculpt, where she is entirely self-taught. After spending around a year creating detailed 3D models and building a strong understanding of digital sculpting, she's now expanding her creativity by learning Blender, one of the world's most powerful and widely used free 3D creation tools.

Although she's relatively new to Blender compared to Nomad Sculpt, Miyabi has adapted remarkably quickly. From modelling and sculpting to experimenting with lighting, materials, and realistic renders, every new project showcases her growing experience and creative talent.

Learning 3D modelling requires patience, imagination, and persistence. Miyabi has already demonstrated those qualities throughout her self-taught journey, and her transition from Nomad Sculpt to Blender is opening up even more creative possibilities.
