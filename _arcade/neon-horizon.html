How to play

  1. Steer across four lanes with the arrow keys or A / D.
  2. Fuel drains constantly — grab the amber canisters to top up.
  3. Traffic gets denser and faster every ten miles.
  4. One collision ends the run. Your best score is saved locally.

On a phone, hold the left or right button to slide between lanes.
