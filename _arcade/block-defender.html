Glitch bugs infect your stack. Crush them with falling blocks, clear lines and collect ⚡ power cells to fire upgrades.

Arrows / WASD · Z or ↑ rotate · Space drop · E upgrade

How to play

  1. Move with ← → and rotate with ↑ or Z. Space hard-drops.
  2. Landing on a glitch bug squashes it for +250.
  3. Full rows clear — but corrupted rows only crack once unless the row holds a power cell.
  4. Power cells arm a row laser or EMP blast. Fire it with E.
  5. Clear the sector target to advance. Five sectors and you win.

Runs locally in your browser. Your best score is saved on this device only.
