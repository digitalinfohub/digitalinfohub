Perfect Pour

How to play

  1. Hold the cup, the button or Space to start pouring.
  2. Release when the coffee sits inside the green band.
  3. The centre of the band earns three stars; the edges earn one.
  4. Each cup pours faster and the target gets tighter. Five cups per shift.

Best shift score is saved in this browser. No account needed.
