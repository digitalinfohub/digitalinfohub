Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Digital Infohub Chrome Extension handles user information.

Information We Collect

The Digital Infohub Chrome Extension does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information or user data.

The extension does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, location data, browsing history, or user activity.

How the Extension Works

The extension provides convenient access to Digital Infohub content through a Chrome browser popup. It may display content from Digital Infohub .

The extension's own code does not collect or transmit user data to Digital Infohub or any third party.

Third-Party Content

Content displayed through the extension may originate from third-party services or websites. Those services may have their own privacy policies and data practices, which are outside the control of the Digital Infohub Chrome Extension.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change.
