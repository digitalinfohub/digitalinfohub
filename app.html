Questions people ask

Is the app different from the website?

No. It is the same Digital Infohub, opened full screen by your browser and cached for offline reading. Everything you see on the site — tools, guides, markets, games, terminal mode — is there.

Does it cost anything or need an account?

It is free and there is nothing to sign up for. The newsletter is optional and separate.

Why is there no App Store or Play Store listing?

The install uses progressive web app support built into modern browsers, so there is no store review, no download size and no update prompt. On iOS the option lives under Share → Add to Home Screen.

What works offline?

Pages you have already opened are cached, along with the core styles and scripts. Tools that calculate locally keep working; anything that needs live prices or AI replies obviously needs a connection.

How do I remove it?

Delete the icon from your home screen or app list, exactly like any other app. Nothing is left behind on the site.
