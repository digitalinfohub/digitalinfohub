Quick answers

How fast will I hear back?

Most messages get a reply within two working days. If your enquiry is about a paid service, include the scope and any deadline so the reply can include a quote.

Can I get a quote before committing?

Yes. Every paid service is scoped and quoted in writing first. You only pay once you approve the scope and price.

Do you take content corrections?

Yes — accuracy matters. Send the article link and the specific correction, and we will review it. AI-assisted content is reviewed by a human and corrected when something is wrong.

Is the form private?

The form is delivered by a form-to-email service straight to the Digital Infohub inbox. Your details are only used to answer your enquiry. Never send passwords or payment details through a contact form.
