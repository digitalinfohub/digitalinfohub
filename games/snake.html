Pixel Palace · Arcade

Snake

The one everybody knows, with a clean board and a saved high score.

How to play

  1. Use the arrow keys or W A S D to steer.
  2. Eat the red dot to grow and add 10 points.
  3. The snake speeds up as you score — plan two moves ahead.
  4. Swipe on a phone; press Space to restart after a crash.

All games run locally: no account, no downloads, nothing uploaded. Scores are saved in this browser only.

Questions

Why does it get faster?

Each dot shortens the gap between moves, up to a floor of 65 ms. It keeps the last minute tense without becoming unplayable.

Is the high score saved?

Yes, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server.
