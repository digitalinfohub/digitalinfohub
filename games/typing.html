How to play

  1. Click the box and start typing the passage.
  2. The timer begins on your first keystroke.
  3. Green is correct, red is a typo — accuracy matters as much as speed.
  4. Your WPM and accuracy appear when the minute ends.

All games run locally: no account, no downloads, nothing uploaded. Scores are saved in this browser only.

Questions

How is WPM calculated?

Characters typed divided by five, then scaled to a minute — the standard word length.

Are mistakes penalised?

They are counted in the accuracy figure. Keep it above 95% before chasing speed.
